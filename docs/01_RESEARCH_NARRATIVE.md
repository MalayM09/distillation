# 01 — Research Narrative: Bridging the Capacity Gap via TAKD

## The Capacity Gap in Edge Medical AI

Deploying segmentation models on portable ultrasound hardware (handheld probes, embedded NPUs, point-of-care tablets) imposes a hard parameter budget — typically under 3M parameters with INT8-quantizable operators. Foundation models such as MedSAM (≈91M parameters, ViT-B backbone) are the de facto state-of-the-art for medical mask generation, yet they are physically inadmissible at the edge: a single forward pass exceeds the SRAM and energy envelope of mobile-class accelerators by more than an order of magnitude. This forces a deployment dichotomy — either run a high-fidelity model in the cloud (incurring latency, privacy, and connectivity penalties) or accept a thin convolutional student trained from scratch on the scarce labels available for medical imaging. Standard knowledge distillation (KD) is the canonical answer to this dichotomy, but our empirical results on BUSI show that direct 91M → 3M distillation does not transfer accuracy; it only damps variance. We call this failure mode the *Capacity Gap*.

## Why Direct 91M → 3M Distillation Fails

The capacity ratio $R = N_T / N_S$ governs whether a student can absorb the teacher's representational manifold. MedSAM encodes lesions in a high-dimensional ViT embedding space (768-dim tokens across 14×14 patches) that is shaped by hundreds of millions of mask-image pairs. A 3M-parameter U-Net possesses neither the channel width nor the receptive-field expressivity to instantiate a function approximator that recovers this manifold; the KL term in the distillation loss collapses to noise relative to the supervised Dice term. At $R \approx 30$, the student's function class $\mathcal{F}_S$ has a Rademacher complexity that is far below the effective complexity of the teacher's output distribution $p_T(y \mid x)$. The student's gradient signal under $\mathcal{L}_{KD}$ degenerates to a per-pixel smoothing prior — a *variance regularizer* that improves calibration but leaves mean Dice statistically unchanged from the supervised-only baseline. This is consistent with the TAKD literature (Mirzadeh et al., 2020) and with the broader observation that distillation gains decay monotonically once $R$ exceeds roughly 8–10×.

## The Goldilocks Ratio: A 15M HA-Net as Teacher Assistant

We close the gap with Teacher Assistant Knowledge Distillation (TAKD): an intermediate domain-expert HA-Net (~15M parameters) is trained from MedSAM and then itself distills into the 3M U-Net. The cascade decomposes the single hard transfer into two well-conditioned hops: $91\text{M} \xrightarrow{R_1 \approx 6} 15\text{M} \xrightarrow{R_2 \approx 5} 3\text{M}$. Both hops sit inside the empirically safe distillation regime ($R \lesssim 8$), so each transfer occurs between models whose function classes are mutually expressible. Geometrically, the assistant acts as a *low-distortion intermediate embedding* — it has enough capacity to faithfully reconstruct MedSAM's semantic manifold while emitting smoothed, ultrasound-specialized targets whose intrinsic dimensionality the 3M student can absorb. Because HA-Net is itself a CNN with hybrid attention, its outputs live in a function space topologically closer to the U-Net than MedSAM's transformer embeddings, eliminating the architectural shock of teacher–student mismatch. The thesis of this work is that this Goldilocks cascade — and only this cascade — converts MedSAM's foundation-model knowledge into measurable edge-time Dice and IoU gains under a strict <3M, INT8-deployable budget.
